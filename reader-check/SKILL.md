# Reader check

An answer can be right and still lost on its reader. This skill reads a finished answer as the person in the reader line and fixes what that person could not follow. It does not change what the answer says or how it is organized.

## Procedure
1. **Get the reader line.** If none is given, say so, then run learning-scope, or assume a non-expert and state that assumption.

2. **Read the answer as that reader and check:**
    - The direct answer to the question asked comes first, not last.
    - Every specialized term is defined in plain words where it first appears.
    - Nothing the reader has not seen in this answer (internal change ids, invariant names, project rituals, prior conversations) is mentioned without a one-phrase reminder of what it is.
    - No bare number is used as evidence without a comparison point the reader has.
    - Any tool or technique the reader line marks as unfamiliar is shown working once on the reader's own material before it is judged.

3. **Fix each failure inline**, keeping the author's content and structure. If the answer came from a subagent, you may send the fixes back to it as a list instead.

4. **Return** the fixed answer and a short list of what changed.

## Guardrails
- Fix only what this reader could not follow; do not rewrite for style or add new content beyond what a check requires.
- Keep fixes short: a definition is a phrase, a reminder is a phrase.
