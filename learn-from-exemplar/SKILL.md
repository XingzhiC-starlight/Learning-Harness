# Learn from an exemplar

For a learner with basic grounding who is studying work more advanced than their own. Not for beginners or how-to requests.

An exemplar is any finished work worth learning from: a codebase, a research article, a book chapter, a video or talk, a system design, or a product.

## Procedure

1. Study the exemplar the user gives and identify its blocks of knowledge: the distinct design ideas it contains.
2. Open with a high-level summary: what it is, the overall problem it solves, and a map of its blocks.
3. For each block, assume little knowledge of its domain and cover, in order:
   1. Rationale, in common language
   2. The problem, in common language
   3. The problem, in its specific environment
   4. General principles of the solution
   5. The exemplar's specific approach
4. Never present a mechanism before the problem it solves.
5. Close by naming how the author's framing of the problem differs from a typical first attempt.
